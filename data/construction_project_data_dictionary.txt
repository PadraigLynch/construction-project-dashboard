Fictional construction-project invoice dataset
================================================
Rows: 288
Period: January 2023 through December 2028
Prime contractor: E-7 (fictional)
Subcontractors: Abrippo, Bopendous, Chabnar, Daptis

Each row represents one monthly invoice submitted by E-7 to a subcontractor.
The Status field is intentionally mixed: most invoices are Paid, while some are Outstanding.
Amounts, dates, and payment timing are simulated and have no connection to real projects.

Suggested dashboard visuals:
- KPI cards: total invoiced, total paid, outstanding balance, invoice count
- Monthly invoiced vs. paid trend
- Invoice amount by vendor
- Outstanding balance by vendor
- Paid vs. outstanding donut/bar chart
- Quarterly or annual spending trend
- Table with invoice details and filters
- Vendor and year/month slicers
